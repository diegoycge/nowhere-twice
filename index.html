Nowhere Twice

A point is walking across a pattern that is perfectly ordered and never repeats.

The pattern is a quasicrystal: a flat slice through a lattice in five dimensions. Every small patch of it turns up again and again across the plane, yet no shift of the whole ever lines up with itself.

Each step along an edge sounds a note. An edge can point in five directions, and they are tuned as a chain of pure fifths, one pitch each. A step forward sounds the upper octave and a step back sounds the lower. Landing on a centre of symmetry adds a bass note.

The point follows three circles turning at golden-ratio rates, plus a slow drift, so its orbit never closes. Nothing is random. Every note is computed from the time since the piece began, at 21:25 UTC on 5 October 2026. Anyone listening at the same moment hears the same thing, and this minute will not come round again.

Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Grid shows the lines the tiling is built from: a note sounds each time the point crosses one. Hidden shows where each corner on screen sits in the dimensions the slice leaves out; the ring marks the centre, where the bass sounds.
